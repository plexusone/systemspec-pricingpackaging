    systemspec-pricingpackaging: Plan

    Initiative: INIT-SYSTEMSPECPRICINGPACKAGING-001 Repository: github.com/plexusone/systemspec-pricingpackaging

    Implements the PRD and TRD. The roadmap holds the work items and their status.

    Approach

    • Small first. The first release covers recurring plans, one-time products, a marketplace fee and the tier-to-entitlement map, and no more. Offers that need more extend the model with examples behind them.
    • Types, then schema, then rules. The Go types are specified and frozen, the schema generated from them, and validation and lint written against the types.
    • Two shapes before freezing. A subscription product with levelled features and a marketplace product both have to be expressible, so the model is not frozen against one.
    • Projections are tested against golden files, so a change to a page, a map or a diff is visible in review.
    • Public-ready from the first commit. Examples are invented; no real products, prices or customers.

    Phases

    Phase Theme Outcome
    1 Design The model checked against two product shapes, the tier and entitlement vocabulary fixed, and the TRD frozen
    2 The model The types, the generated schema, validation and lint
    3 Helpers and projections Plan, feature and limit helpers; the pricing view, entitlement map and diff
    4 Examples and release Invented examples for two shapes, a guide, and v0.1.0

    Order and dependencies

    Phase 1 gates the rest. Phase 3 depends on the types from Phase 2. The billing library that consumes the spec is planned in its own repository and starts once the types are frozen.

    Verification

    • Validation and lint: a test for each rule.
    • Helpers: unknown identifiers, level ordering, unlimited versus zero.
    • Projections: golden files.
    • Schema: generated, linted with the family's tool, and used to validate the examples.
    • A billing library imports the package and builds a pricing view, a limit check and a provider plan from an example before v0.1.0.
