# v0.2.0

**Release Date:** 2026-11-11

Internal plans: a plan for the operator's own accounts, such as staff and
test accounts, that an operator assigns by hand and that is never sold or
shown.

## Highlights

- **A plan that is never offered.** Mark a plan `internal` and the pricing
  view leaves it out, while `Plan`, `Has` and `Check` still resolve it for the
  accounts on it, so they get its features and limits like any other plan.
- **Validated.** An internal plan has no price, is not self-serve and is not
  the default plan (`E014`).
- **Visible in a diff.** `Diff` reports a plan that becomes internal, or stops
  being internal.

## Upgrading

The change is an addition: a Spec without `internal` reads and behaves as
before. A host that renders a pricing page from `PricingView` needs no change
to keep internal plans off it.
